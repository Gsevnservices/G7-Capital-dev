01 · The Problem

Get your financials audited internally —
before your external auditor does.

Most companies meet their auditor once a year — after the books are closed. By then, a missed approval or a wrong treatment is no longer a fix. It is a qualification, a CARO remark, or a penalty notice with the CFO's and directors' names on it.

Found too late
Issues surface at year-end audit, when they can only be disclosed — not prevented.
Personal liability
Accounting-standard breaches flow into the Directors' Responsibility Statement. Penalties land on the company, the CFO and the directors.
The knowledge gap
Ind AS, CARO, Section 188, IFC — the rules are complex, and most finance teams have no auditor sitting with them.
